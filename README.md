## About Me

Mission. That is what I value most in every aspect of my life. After all, apart from mission, a project is empty and lifeless. However, when a group of people are passionate about the mission they pursue, anything can be accomplished.

This value shapes how I approach my career. Not every software engineer needs to be passionate about the mission served by their work, but I do.

Having grandparents who served in the Air Force and Marine Corps, I learned from a young age that the United States of America is the greatest nation on Earth and is worth defending. As we have entered the digital age, the methods of this defense have adapted, and at the forefront of this is the United States Space Force, with the stated mission to “Secure our Nation’s Interests In, From, And To Space.”

I’m currently a junior at the Colorado School of Mines, pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a minor in Applied Mathematics and Statistics, to be followed by a Master of Science in Computer Science in one additional year via the combined program. Through this I am continually growing in my knowledge and understanding of software engineering through an education that teaches far more than just syntax but also emphasizes the best practices to design secure and efficient programs. In my coursework, I have also learned that the software I get most excited to develop is user-facing, working on programs that people will interact with directly.

I also work at the Colorado School of Mines IT Service Desk, where I became a Training Lead my second semester employed and a Student Manager my third semester. As a Student Manager, I am organizing and leading a new team at the Service Desk that takes on an active workplace mentoring role, supporting our fellow student workers. Through additional training, these team members also receive elevated access to the systems we use like Jamf Pro and Active Directory, to better support our troubleshooting efforts. 

I am pursuing a career in the defense and space sector supporting a mission I am truly passionate about: developing software that will be used by the Guardians of the United States Space Force.
